## Remaining limitations

This implements an in-process first-install component with fixed echo/unchanged-consumer readiness, not a general standalone service. The CLI's install/start/stop/upgrade/verify/backup commands remain unavailable. Candidate runtime/SDK assembly is not used in place of the trusted development runtime. Total lifecycle deadline enforcement still needs work/review; individual Kernel operation bounds do not prove the whole Cell deadline. Structured corruption/durability uncertainty outcomes, clean-failure same-object usability, independently authenticated post-crash reap/restart, upgrade/reinstall, supervisor/admin transport, full runtime inventory/SBOM, age backup/restore and independent two-host qualification remain incomplete. Successful component tests do not qualify these missing behaviors.
